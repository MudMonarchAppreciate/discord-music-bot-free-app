## 🌟 About

**Discord Music Bot Free App** is a lightweight, fully open-source music bot that plays audio in your Discord voice channels — **completely free, forever**. No premium tiers, no locked features, no hidden limits.

It supports YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, and direct URL playback, with a clean slash-command interface, a full-featured queue system, and rich embeds showing album art and playback progress.

> 💡 **Perfect for:** Small communities, friend servers, gaming groups, and anyone who wants shared music without paying for a bot subscription.
